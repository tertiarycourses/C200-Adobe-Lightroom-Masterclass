# Activity 02 - Marina Bay Event Library: Cull, Organise, and Retrieve

**Course:** Adobe Lightroom Masterclass
**Course Code:** C200
**Alignment:** Topic 1

## Scenario

A hospitality group has 16 event photographs from a launch night. Marketing needs six images that show people, venue atmosphere, product detail, and a clean hero frame. The library must support later retrieval by campaign, subject, location, and approval status without duplicating originals.
