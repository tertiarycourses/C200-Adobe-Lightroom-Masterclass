# Activity 07 - Capstone: Northstar Retail Campaign Review and Delivery

**Course:** Adobe Lightroom Masterclass
**Course Code:** C200
**Alignment:** Topic 3
**Version:** v1.0 | 30 September 2026

## Acceptance checklist

- [ ] Every brief, edit, rights and delivery decision is traceable
- [ ] Unverified assets are quarantined
- [ ] Edits align to the brief and design principles
- [ ] Shared access follows least privilege
- [ ] Location/privacy and AI disclosure decisions are explicit
- [ ] All exports pass destination checks
- [ ] Another editor can follow the decision trail

## Universal production checks

- [ ] The source images remain unchanged.
- [ ] The edit has a stated purpose linked to the brief.
- [ ] Before/after and critical 100% inspections are captured.
- [ ] Masks, retouching, and AI-assisted areas show no visible artefacts.
- [ ] Rights, consent, privacy, and material AI disclosure are addressed.
- [ ] Exported files meet the destination specification and open correctly.
- [ ] Evidence is complete and suitable for trainer review.
